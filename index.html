      Up and running in 3 minutes.

      1. Download

        Get Khanjar.zip from the latest release on GitHub, unzip it and drag Khanjar.app into Applications.

      2. Allow the first launch

        The beta is not signed by Apple yet. In System Settings, Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. You do it once.

      3. Let it set itself up

        Click Allow when macOS asks about your Documents folder, where your presets live. Khanjar then installs its Premiere plugin through the Creative Cloud app.

      4. Press ⌘J in Premiere

        Select clips, press ⌘J, type a few letters, press Enter.

      Questions editors ask.

      Does it work with the preset packs I bought?add
      Yes. Khanjar reads your Premiere Effect Presets, where installed packs live, and Adobe's own presets, even presets saved in a Premiere set to another language. Third-party effects used by a pack must be installed.
      Do animated presets keep their timing?add
      Yes. Keyframes follow the preset's anchoring, stretched to the clip or pinned to its start or end, and eased curves are reproduced frame by frame.
      Does it slow Premiere down?add
      No. An effect is applied in about 5 to 15 ms, and Khanjar does nothing until you press a shortcut.
      What can't it do yet?add
      These limits come from what Adobe's plugin API allows today:
      • Presets built on a mask, or on Lumetri curves, color wheels or LUTs, are left out rather than applied wrong. Lumetri presets that only move sliders work.
      • It can't create an adjustment layer. Make one once in your project, Khanjar reuses it.
      • Audio effects and transitions are not supported yet.
      Is it free? Windows? Intel Macs?add
      Free and open source (GPL-3.0). macOS on Apple Silicon with Premiere Pro 2026 only, for now.
      Does it send anything online?add
      Khanjar runs on your Mac and talks to Premiere over a local connection. Update checks are optional, crash reports are off unless you turn them on, and never include your clips, projects or file paths.
